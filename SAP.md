### What is "Inclusive Workforce"?
This theme focuses on how AI can bridge the $5.5 trillion skills gap and make hiring fair for everyone, especially those at the margins (tier-2/3 city talent, first-generation job seekers, people with career breaks, and persons with disabilities). 

SAP wants teams to build a **"Career Orchestrator"** — a multi-agent AI system that looks beyond traditional resumes and focuses on true skills, matching candidates fairly to jobs and suggesting personalized learning pathways.

### 3. Which Target Group (Persona) Should You Choose?
You asked a very important question: *"The theme mentions multiple things like first-gen, disabilities, women, rural, displaced workers. Do I have to solve all of them?"*

**The Answer is NO.** 
The hackathon rules clearly state in Round 1: *"Teams select **an** inclusion challenge"*. You only need to pick **ONE** target audience. Your project is already perfectly built to solve **two** of these challenges:

**Option A (Highly Recommended): Rural and Tier-2/3 Talent / First-generation job seekers**
- **The Problem:** Companies hire from Tier-1 colleges because they trust the "brand". A brilliant coder from a Tier-3 city gets rejected because their resume doesn't have the right keywords or college name.
- **How ReadyIndex solves this:** Your platform ignores the college name. It looks at their **GitHub** (real code) and **LeetCode** (problem-solving skills). It parses their projects using AI and generates a "ReadyIndex Score". The AI matches them to jobs based on what they *can* do, not where they come from.

**Option B: Women returning to work (after a career break)**
- **The Problem:** ATS software automatically rejects resumes with 2-3 years of employment gaps (often due to maternity leave or caregiving).
- **How ReadyIndex solves this:** Your AI parser evaluates *present capabilities*. If a woman took a 3-year break but recently completed projects or learned Next.js, your AI sees those skills and matches her to jobs based on *current* skills, ignoring the gap.

*Conclusion: In your pitch deck, clearly state that your Career OS focuses on solving the bias against Tier-2/3 talent and those with unconventional backgrounds.*

### 4. How to pitch your project in Round 1
