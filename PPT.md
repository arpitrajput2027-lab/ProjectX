## Slide 3: PROBLEM STATEMENT
### Hiring Is Slow for Recruiters and Unclear for Students

Recruiters may receive **1,000+ applications for one role**, making manual screening slow, expensive and inconsistent. A single hire can involve approximately **₹15,000–₹35,000 in hiring cost**, depending on the process and resources involved.

At the same time, students do not have an objective way to understand whether their resume, projects and coding skills match a particular job. They often apply without knowing their skill gaps, ATS weaknesses or interview readiness.

### Core Problem

**Recruiters cannot quickly identify the right skill-based candidates, while students cannot clearly understand or improve their readiness for a specific job.**

### Why Existing Information Is Not Enough

- Resume shows claims, but not always practical proof
- GitHub, LeetCode and resumes are checked separately
- Generic career advice is not based on the student's actual profile
- Students apply blindly to jobs that may not match their current skills

**Suggested visual:** Many scattered resumes, GitHub and LeetCode pages on one side, with a confused student and overloaded recruiter in the center.

## Slide 4: SOLUTION
### ReadyIndex: One Intelligent Profile for Better Career Decisions

ReadyIndex combines a student's resume, GitHub, LeetCode, career goals and job requirements into one AI-powered career profile.

### How It Solves the Problem

- **AI Resume Parsing:** Converts a PDF resume into structured education, experience, projects and skills.
- **Real-Time GitHub and LeetCode Integration:** Adds practical coding evidence and learning activity.
- **ATS Checker:** Evaluates formatting, keywords, readability and resume structure.
- **Job Description Matcher:** Shows match score, missing skills and role-specific gaps.
- **Explainable ReadyIndex Score:** Combines multiple signals and shows exactly why the score was given.
- **RAG Resume Chat:** Answers questions using the student's own resume, projects and target job.
- **AI Mock Interview Coach:** Creates role-specific interview questions and improvement feedback.
- **Recruiter Workspace:** Supports talent discovery, bulk resume processing, candidate review and interview management.

### Product Journey

**Connect -> Analyze -> Explain -> Practice -> Improve -> Match -> Apply**
